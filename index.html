01 / ABOUT

From software development to IT infrastructure.

I am an IT Support Technician at Vuxeni Information Technologies with more than three years of practical experience supporting users, workstations, network-connected devices and enterprise printer environments across multiple client sites.

My day-to-day work covers first- and second-line support, Windows troubleshooting, hardware and software support, printer installation and maintenance, TCP/IP connectivity, structured cabling, remote assistance and onsite technical support.

I hold a National Diploma in Information Technology (Software Development) from Tshwane University of Technology. That background complements my support experience by giving me a systems-oriented approach to troubleshooting: understand the environment, isolate the fault, test the solution and document the outcome.

My career direction is toward infrastructure and network engineering, while continuing to develop practical skills in Linux, systems administration, automation and cloud technologies.
